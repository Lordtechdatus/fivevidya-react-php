- Existing pages retained: 13 (Home plus 12 service pages)

- Misdirected links fixed: 33 (17 submenu links, 15 footer links and the header Contact link)
- Remaining missing pages: 0
- New major service pages: 838–936 words, with four specific FAQs each
- Category overview pages: focused pathway descriptions and relevant navigation
